# CinaCLAW AI — 60-Second Advertising Promo Blueprint

## Creative brief

The promo is a **16:9 cinematic technology commercial** for tech-savvy consumers ages 18–30. It uses restrained filmic contrast, cool graphite backgrounds, electric cyan accents, shallow depth-of-field transitions, gentle camera drift over real product screenshots, and fade-in/fade-out bookends. The visual treatment must preserve the actual CinaCLAW interface and must not invent controls or claim unsupported capabilities.

The call to action is: **Visit the Cina-Claw-Pro v1.0.4 release on GitHub.**

## Voice profile

Use English, United States regional voice guidance, and a **slow, deep, resonant male voice with a tasteful Southern drawl**. The performance should feel confident, warm, and cinematic rather than comedic or exaggerated. Keep consonants clear, leave short breaths between feature phrases, and avoid rushing the final CTA. Suggested catalog voice: **Algieba** for a smooth male foundation, with the direction below applied:

> Speak in English with a slow, deep, resonant male commercial voice and a subtle, tasteful Southern drawl. Sound confident, warm, and cinematic; never caricature the accent. Maintain clear diction and measured pauses for a technology advertisement aimed at young adult tech enthusiasts: Meet CinaCLAW. [short pause] A Windows-first autonomous multimodal AI command center, powered by OpenClaw. Bring chat, images, files, voice, skills, browser tools, channels, and scheduled automation into one focused desktop experience. Ask a question, delegate the work, and keep control of the details. With free-first model options, local Ollama support, encrypted provider credentials, and approval-aware host commands, CinaCLAW is built to be capable without getting in your way. Version one point zero four is live for Windows, with audited skills and cross-platform validation behind it. Take a look at the release, and put your next idea in motion.

Approximate spoken length: 111 words. The intended slow commercial delivery should land close to 55–60 seconds; trim pauses before cutting factual content.

## Editorial and evidence rules

Use real repository screenshots for every interface shot. Cinematic movement means crop, pan, zoom, light, and transition only; it must not redraw or modify interface controls. The release claim is supported by the public v1.0.4 release page and the completed Windows workflow. The promo should not claim Android or iOS availability for CinaCLAW, and it should not imply that free model availability, external provider quotas, or unsigned Windows installer behavior are permanent guarantees.
